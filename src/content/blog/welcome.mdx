Welcome! This is the blog of the Astro Starter Template. It works the same way the blog on your own site will: every post is a Markdown or MDX file in `src/content/blog/`, and it appears in the list automatically. It is also added to the [RSS feed](/rss.xml) and gets its own social image.

## Looking for the guides?

The guides for setting up and customizing the template are in the [docs](/docs). Good places to start:

- [How to make this template yours](/docs/how-to-configure-config)
- [How to create a new page](/docs/how-to-create-a-new-page)
- [Markdown style guide](/docs/markdown-style-guide)

## Write your first post

Create a file such as `src/content/blog/my-first-post.mdx`:

```mdx
---
title: My first post
description: A short introduction to my first blog post.
pubDate: 2026-11-18
---

Write your post here.
```

The post will appear at `/blog/my-first-post`. Once your own posts are in place, edit or delete this one.
